How to sharpen a kitchen knife

By Mara Chen — 4 October 2026

A short guide to sharpening a kitchen knife at home with a whetstone, in five steps.

Soak the stone

Put the whetstone in water for ten minutes, until no more bubbles rise.

Hold the angle

Hold the blade at about 15 degrees to the stone and keep that angle steady.

Work both sides

Push the blade across the stone, then turn it over and do the same on the other side.

Check the edge

Run your thumb lightly across the edge, away from the sharp side, to feel for a burr.

Wash and dry

Rinse the knife, dry it at once, and put it away.
